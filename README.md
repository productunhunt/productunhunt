# PRODUCT UNHUNT

**The internet has enough serious people building serious products to solve
serious problems. ProductUnhunt is for the rest of us.**

[productunhunt.com](https://productunhunt.com)

</div>

---

ProductUnhunt is a board of business ideas that were considered and then
correctly abandoned.

Every idea gets the full treatment — a product name, a savage tagline, five
sections of straight-faced analysis, a byline — and then it stays unbuilt.
That is the entire point. The presentation is serious precisely because the
idea is not.

This is not a launch platform. It is not a startup graveyard, which would
imply these things ever lived. It is not somewhere to park a pitch deck. Every
idea here was dead before it arrived, and the analysis is a post-mortem on a
patient who was never admitted.

## What's on it

**Recently not built** — the feed. Newest regrets first.

**The full pile** — every idea ever filed, by tag.

**Community verdict** — six options, one vote per idea, no account required.
Changeable, if you come to your senses.

> Deserves a Trophy for Ridiculous · Should Stay Unbuilt · Accidentally Genius
> · Founder needs sleep · Someone will build this · Unfortunately, I'd pay

**Comments** — for roasting the idea. Not the author.

## Statuses

| Badge                   | What it means                    |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Regrettably unbuilt** | The default, and the ideal       |
| **Unfortunately real**  | Someone went and built it anyway |
| **Do not encourage**    | Still a threat                   |

The second one is rare and editor-assigned, and always comes with a short note
about how it became real. Nobody is proud of those.

## You have bad ideas too

Two doors, same format.

**Open a pull request.** Copy [`IDEA_TEMPLATE.md`](./IDEA_TEMPLATE.md), fill
it in, add it as `src/content/ideas/your-slug.md`.

**Email it.** Send the same fields as plain text to
**ideas@productunhunt.com** and let someone else do the YAML.

The bar is funny and specific. Real pitch decks, attacks on a person, and
another empty "Uber for X" get rejected without ceremony. Full details in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

The entry requirement is one line, and it is not negotiable:

> "I thought about building this and chose not to."

## The deal

This is entertainment. Treat every idea as public and already dead.

By submitting, you grant ProductUnhunt a perpetual licence to publish, edit,
and roast it. You keep whatever rights you think you had. We promise nothing.

Do not send confidential plans, client work, or anything you would mind seeing
next to _Founder needs sleep_.

Published ideas may be copy-edited. The joke stays yours; the commas may not.
We can reject or unpublish anything, no debate.
